Worked examples

How a prediction is produced

The engine applies the two static models named in the FDA 2020 drug interaction guidance and ICH M12: a basic screen that discards most candidate edges cheaply, then a mechanistic static model that returns a predicted exposure ratio. Potency alone never decides anything.

1 · Entity resolution

The query entity is a preparation — species, plant part, extract type and daily dose — because root and leaf of one species are different chemical inputs. Drugs resolve to moieties, so S-warfarin and R-warfarin are scored separately against their own clearance routes.

2 · The abundance gate

A constituent enters the arithmetic only if it has a quantified abundance in that plant part and preparation, or is a pharmacopoeial marker constituent. Everything else is counted and listed but never scored. Without this gate, a species with hundreds of catalogued compounds eventually flags on CYP3A4 no matter what it contains.

3 · Exposure tiering

Gut lumen concentration needs only the dose and the weight fraction, using the 250 mL convention:

[I]gut = (D_preparation × w_constituent) / (250 mL × MW)

Hepatic inlet unbound concentration needs systemic pharmacokinetics, which for most phytochemicals does not exist:

[I]h,u = fu,p × ( Cmax + (Fa · Fg · ka · D) / (Qh · RB) )
  • Tier A — human Cmax and a curated absorbed fraction. Point estimate, both compartments.
  • Tier B — Cmax known, absorption unknown. An interval between systemic unbound and a fully-absorbed inlet bound. Severity from the worst end, confidence from the width.
  • Tier C — dose and abundance only. Intestinal terms compute; hepatic terms are suppressed, not guessed.
  • Tier D — nothing to anchor on. No ratio, no severity. The causal chain is shown as a hypothesis.

4 · Typed bioactivity

Three separate edge types, because they need different parameters and behave differently: reversible inhibition (Ki), time-dependent inactivation (KI and kinact) and induction (Emax and EC50). Duplicate assays are resolved before summation, never added twice.

5 · Basic static screen

R1 = 1 + [I]u / Ki R1,gut = 1 + [I]gut / Ki R3 = 1 / (1 + d·Emax·[I] / (EC50 + [I]))

An edge proceeds at R1 ≥ 1.02 hepatic, R1,gut ≥ 11 intestinal, or R3 ≤ 0.8 for induction. These are the guideline cut-offs, so the screen is citable rather than invented.

6 · Mechanistic static model

AUCR = 1 / ( [Ag·Bg·Cg·(1 − Fg) + Fg] × Π_enzymes [Ah·Bh·Ch·fm + (1 − fm)] )
A = 1 / (1 + Σ [I]/Ki) B = kdeg / (kdeg + Σ kinact·[I]/(KI + [I])) C = 1 + d·Emax·[I]/(EC50 + [I])

Reversible inhibition is summed across constituents because competitive inhibition at a shared site is additive. Inactivation sums at the level of rate. Induction is deliberately not summed — co-induction is not reliably additive, and pretending otherwise would repeat the error this model exists to fix.

For a prodrug the engine reports an activation ratio instead, since the hazard is too little active drug:

AMR = Σ_e ( Ah·Bh·Ch · f_act,e ) + ( 1 − Σ_e f_act,e )

7 · Severity and confidence

Severity comes from the predicted ratio and the drug's graded therapeutic index. Confidence comes from the evidence behind that ratio. They are separate axes and either can be low while the other is high.

SeverityCondition
MAJORAUCR ≥ 2 on an NTI drug, AUCR ≥ 5 on any drug, or activation ratio ≤ 0.5 where loss of effect is itself dangerous
MODERATEAUCR 1.25–2 on an NTI drug, AUCR 2–5 otherwise, or 0.2–0.5 on a non-critical drug
MINORAUCR 1.25–2 on a non-NTI drug, or 0.5–0.8 on a non-critical drug
NONEAUCR between 0.8 and 1.25, the guideline no-effect boundary

Three confidence caps are structural rather than data-dependent. Transporter-mediated signals cap at LOW, because in vitro efflux and uptake inhibition translate poorly to clinical exposure. Prodrug predictions cap at MODERATE, because static models cannot represent formation-rate-limited metabolite kinetics. Any prediction touching a placeholder parameter caps at LOW and is marked provisional.

What this engine does not do

Transporter edges are reported as co-signals and are never folded into the ratio. Pharmacodynamic interactions are out of scope entirely — the output quantity is an exposure ratio, which has no pharmacodynamic analogue. Genotype, pregnancy, paediatric and organ-impairment adjustments are not modelled, and absorption-enhancing matrix effects are not represented. Polyherbal formulations resolve one preparation at a time.

Reproducibility

Every scored result carries an audit record containing the engine version, the data version, the full parameter set and the physiological constants. Re-running that record reproduces the number exactly. Without it, calling the pipeline deterministic would be unverifiable.
